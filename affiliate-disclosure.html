Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure

Last updated: 21 November 2026

MarketMantra does not currently have active affiliate links or paid sponsorships in this starter version. If that changes, this page will be updated and a clear disclosure such as “Affiliate”, “Ad” or “Sponsored” will be placed prominently near the relevant link or promotion.

How affiliate links may work

If we join an affiliate programme, MarketMantra may receive a commission when a visitor makes a qualifying purchase or completes another qualifying action through a tracked link. This should not increase the visitor's price unless the offer clearly says otherwise. Programme-specific terms may apply.

Editorial independence and risks

Compensation can create a conflict of interest. We will aim to explain relevant commercial relationships and avoid presenting paid placement as independent advice. A commission is not a guarantee that a product is suitable, safe or the best option. Visitors should read the provider's terms and independently assess products.

Advertising

Third-party advertisements may appear if an advertising service is added in future. Advertisers are responsible for their offers and claims. We will not knowingly make misleading claims about returns or financial products.

All disclosures should be clear, prominent and understandable, and the operator should check applicable ASCI, consumer protection, platform and programme requirements before publishing promotions.
