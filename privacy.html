Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy describes the current starter version of MarketMantra. It must be reviewed and updated before adding analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking, forms, comments, accounts or other third-party services.

1. Who operates this website?

MarketMantra is an educational website project. Before public launch, the operator must add their correct legal name or business name and a working contact email on the Contact page. Do not publish placeholder contact details.

2. Information entered into calculators

The SIP, EMI and FD calculators in this version perform calculations in your browser. The values you enter are not intentionally sent to a MarketMantra server by the calculator code. Do not enter bank account numbers, passwords, card details, account credentials or other sensitive personal information.

3. Hosting and technical information

This website is intended to be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs to provide, secure and operate hosting. Please review GitHub's current privacy information for details of its processing. MarketMantra does not control GitHub's infrastructure.

4. Third-party services

The current version does not include a working newsletter submission, user account system, payment gateway, analytics tag or advertising tag. If these are added later, this policy must be updated to identify the providers, purposes, data involved, retention and applicable choices or consent mechanisms. External websites linked from MarketMantra have their own privacy practices.

5. Cookies and advertising

No MarketMantra advertising or analytics cookies are intentionally set by the current website code. Hosting or linked third-party services may operate independently. If advertising, analytics or affiliate tracking is introduced, provide appropriate notices and controls where required by applicable law and the provider's terms.

6. Your choices and requests

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact details added by the website operator on the Contact page. The operator should respond in accordance with applicable law. Do not include sensitive financial credentials in a message.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when website features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Launch checklist: Add the operator's real contact details; review the actual hosting and third-party services enabled; and obtain legal advice on obligations applicable to the site's real data practices.
